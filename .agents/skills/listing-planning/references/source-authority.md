# Source authority and planning truth

## Purpose

Planning must distinguish product fact, commercial decision, marketing decision, consumer evidence, localization reference, channel evidence, and visual reference before any consumer message is locked.

## Authority order

For product fact, prefer the latest final/approved specification, test result, certification, or explicit product decision over older planning or marketing material. For commercial and marketing decisions, prefer explicit current user/project decisions over historical drafts.

A newer source does not automatically outrank a more authoritative source. Record version/date, scope, owner, and downstream dependency.

## Fact states

Use planning states such as `CONFIRMED`, `CONDITIONAL`, `CONFLICT`, `MISSING`, and `PROHIBITED`.

A conflict must stay visible until a higher-authority source resolves it. Do not blend conflicting values into one consumer claim.

## Claim readiness

A capability can exist as a product fact without being ready for a marketing claim. Separate:

- mechanism fact;
- measured performance;
- comparison claim;
- launch-scope decision;
- legal/compliance readiness.

External market evidence can support terminology, use cases, objections, and purchase motivation. It cannot prove a product capability.

## Offer and page boundary

Keep offer/page scope explicit. A fact or claim that belongs to one offer must not silently leak into another page target.

## Output

Planning should maintain a concise Fact/Conflict/Claim baseline that can support Consumer Strategy and the Production Handoff without carrying the entire source archive downstream.
