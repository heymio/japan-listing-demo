# Localization planning

## Purpose

Locale work converts evidence-backed strategy into language and framing appropriate to the target locale without inventing needs from the market label alone.

## Locale boundary

For `ja-JP`, use current Japanese market/channel evidence and natural Japanese copy conventions. Do not assume a preference, pain point, or scenario merely because the project market is Japan.

## What localization may change

Localization may adapt:

- terminology and category language;
- sentence structure and tone;
- shopper questions and objection framing;
- search language supported by current evidence;
- examples and scenarios supported by market/channel evidence;
- information density and emphasis where channel/local expectations are evidenced.

## What localization may not change

Localization may not alter product fact, offer scope, claim readiness, measured performance, or channel capability.

## Evidence

Keep locale evidence traceable to current sources. Separate stable language conventions from volatile market/search behavior.

## Output

Produce a Localization Brief that informs Message Architecture and the Creative Strategy Kernel. Production receives only the locale implications relevant to the current asset, not the entire localization research history.
