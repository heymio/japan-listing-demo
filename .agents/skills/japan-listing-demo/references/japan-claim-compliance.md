# Japan claim and compliance verification

## Purpose

This reference creates a verification queue. It is not legal advice and does not declare that one rule applies to every product.

Applicable requirements depend on the current category, product design, service model, data flow, sales method, channel, audience, and release scope.

## Verification queue

For each project, determine whether current authoritative sources are required for:

- product safety, certification, testing, installation, and labeling;
- wireless, electrical, battery, or connected-device requirements;
- advertising representations, comparisons, rankings, endorsements, and superiority claims;
- pricing, discounts, tax, subscription, renewal, cancellation, and trial terms;
- warranties, returns, delivery, availability, and seller identity;
- privacy, account data, analytics, recordings, biometrics, cloud processing, and cross-border transfer;
- children, health, accessibility, or other protected-use contexts;
- environmental, recycling, packaging, and disposal information;
- marketplace-specific restricted claims and content policies.

Only retain the rows relevant to the current project.

## Evidence hierarchy

Use this order:

1. current official government, regulator, standards, or platform source;
2. current approved certification, test, legal, or compliance record;
3. current signed commercial or service terms;
4. current company policy approved for the target project;
5. secondary explanation for orientation only.

Do not rely on an earlier project, another category, a competitor page, or an old marketplace implementation as proof.

## Claim record

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| claim | Exact consumer-visible statement or visual implication |
| evidence | Source and version supporting it |
| conditions | Test, environment, compatibility, account, subscription, or use conditions |
| market / locale | Scope actually supported |
| channel | Channel policy and editable slot |
| owner | Product, legal, compliance, commercial, or other approver |
| status | `LOCKED`, `PENDING CLAIM`, `PROHIBITED`, or `INTERNAL ONLY` |
| refresh trigger | Date, product change, policy change, or new evidence |

## Writing rule

Conditions must stay attached to the statement they qualify. A footnote cannot repair a headline whose main meaning is broader than the evidence.

A visual can create a claim even when the text does not. Review copy, icons, diagrams, comparisons, before/after scenes, badges, and UI together.

## Release gate

Formal consumer use requires:

- authoritative evidence;
- current channel-policy compatibility;
- required product and commercial approval;
- locale review;
- the same conditions across headline, body, visual, comparison, and Q&A;
- a documented refresh trigger.

Anything else remains review-only.
